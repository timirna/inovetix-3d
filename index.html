Is Inovetix the right fit for my business?

Whether you are an early-stage startup needing an MVP or an established enterprise scaling operations, our cross-functional engineering and design teams adapt to your goals.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. We can operate as an autonomous end-to-end product squad or integrate directly into your in-house engineering and product teams.

Do you handle both design and development?

Yes. This includes user research, UX/UI design, interactive prototypes, design systems, frontend and backend engineering, cloud architecture and post-launch maintenance.

How do projects usually start?

Every project begins with a discovery and alignment phase. From there, we define a clear roadmap, establish the team and begin sprint execution within days.

How are timelines and pricing set?

Both depend on scope. Larger programs run on a monthly retainer or a dedicated sprint squad, and pricing is tied to your milestones.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes. We provide continuous support, monitoring, security updates, feature iterations and performance tuning after your product goes live.
