Questions

Is Inovetix the right fit for my business?

We suit companies that want to build, modernize or scale a digital product, from an early-stage startup needing an MVP to an established enterprise scaling operations.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. We can run as a complete product squad or join your in-house engineering and product teams, using your tools, channels and sprint rituals.

Do you handle both design and development?

Yes. That covers user research, UX and UI design, prototypes, design systems, frontend and backend engineering, cloud architecture and post-launch maintenance.

How do projects usually start?

With a discovery and alignment phase. We go through your requirements, architecture and milestones, define a roadmap, name the team and begin sprints within days.

How are timelines and pricing set?

Both depend on scope. Larger programs run on a monthly retainer or a dedicated sprint squad, and pricing is tied to your milestones.

Do you support products after launch?

Yes. We provide DevOps monitoring, security updates, feature iterations and performance tuning after your product goes live.
